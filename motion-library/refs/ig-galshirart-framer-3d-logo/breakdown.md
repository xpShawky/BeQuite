# Framer brand-identity reel (galshirart × Framer): breakdown

**File:** `inbox/New/Instagram.mp4` (original name) · **Source:** Instagram, galshirart × Framer collab (https://www.instagram.com/p/DPjOElAjJaV/, identified from the Framer logo/wordmark on screen and the wishlist order) · **Length / format:** 16.1 s, 1080x1920, 60 fps · **Studied from:** shots.jpg, matches.jpg, a 4 fps contact sheet (`frames/c_sheet.jpg`), summary.md, director.md
**Category:** brand film · 3D logo · kinetic type

## 1. The story in one line
The Framer mark is drawn like a design file, turned into a lit 3D object, set as a wordmark and colour system, and ends stitched on a cap: "the brand, built in front of you".

## 2. Story shape
Construction (vector) → material (3D + light) → system (wordmark, palette) → application (merch). A brand-guideline deck told as motion; no voice, no CTA.

## 4. Signature techniques
- **Transitions:** 9 hard cuts; 7 tagged graphic-match, 6 position-locked (summary). The logo/wordmark stays at the same centre point across every ground change. No dissolves.
- **Typography:** one geometric sans wordmark; first loose tracking that tightens, then an oversized crop. No other copy.
- **Camera:** locked-off 69 % of time; motion is inside the objects (rotation, light blade).
- **Colour and background:** Framer blue #0055FF / #0099FF, white, black, grey: the palette shown at 10.6 s is the palette used for every ground.
- **Pacing:** ASL 1.61 s, curve [2.01, 2.01, 0.8, 1.01]: long material shot, then rapid swaps.
- **Sound:** music + hits, −14.2 LUFS, 117.5 BPM (low confidence 2.31). Cuts on strong onsets 11 % vs chance 14 % (lift ×0.81): the cuts are not driven by the beat; hits sit at about 1 s intervals.

## 5. When to use this style
- Fits: brand/identity launches, design-tool or dev-tool brands with a simple mark; 10-20 s vertical.
- Avoid when: the product needs explaining; the mark is complex (extrusion turns muddy).
- Cost: medium. Vector build in AE (shape layers + cursor), 3D extrusion and glass in Blender/C4D (or AE with Element 3D), cap mockup render. ~2-4 days.

## 6. What to steal
1. **Build the logo with a fake cursor** in the opening 2 s, and bring the cursor back at the end (bookend).
2. **Light-blade reveal**: a thin diagonal light bar crosses the flat mark and leaves it extruded.
3. **Position-locked ground swaps**: hold the mark still, cut the background through the brand palette at 0.5 s.
4. **Palette fan-out** as one 1.5 s beat instead of a static guideline slide.
5. For BeQuite: build the BeQuite mark as if typed/drawn in a terminal, then make it a glass object.
