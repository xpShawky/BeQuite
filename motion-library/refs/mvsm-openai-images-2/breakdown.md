## 4. Signature techniques
- **Transitions:** 77 hard cuts, no effects. Graphic-match tags (42) are mostly false positives from repeated stills; the real ones are subject-locked swaps (49.71, 50.01 s). One white flash.
- **Typography:** zero overlaid type. Every word is diegetic: printed, embroidered, neon, magnets, cereal, signage. Fonts change every shot; the sentence is the continuity.
- **Camera:** stills, 73 % locked-off; inner motion 25 % (small pushes, stutter frames).
- **Colour and background:** photographic, saturated, green as the hero hue (chameleon) in almost every frame; white open and close.
- **Pacing:** ASL 0.77 s, median 0.67 s, 76.9 cuts/min; curve [1.07, 0.83, 0.54, 0.71]: accelerates into the adventure montage, eases at the end.
- **Sound:** a licensed song carries everything, −12.9 LUFS, LRA 3.3 LU, ≈ 96 BPM (low confidence). Cuts are paced to reading, not beats: on-onset 19 % vs chance 18 % (lift ×1.08). The sung vocal is the only voice; it is not translated or used as narration.

## 5. When to use this style
- Fits: AI image/video models, design tools, anything whose output is visual; a capability you can show in every frame; 30–60 s; social and launch pages.
- Avoid when: the product is not visual output; when you cannot make 70+ consistent images; serious B2B tone.
- Cost and difficulty: medium. The work is a written word-per-shot script, then image generation (dozens of prompts per word to get clean text and one consistent character), then a fast edit. AI images are allowed here because the product IS AI images.

## 6. What to steal (adapt, never copy)
1. **Found-type sentence:** the tagline spelled across objects in the world (for BeQuite: words on a terminal, a sticky note, a folder label, a commit message, a gate badge).
2. **The demo is the story:** every frame is product output, so no separate "feature" shots are needed.
3. **Subject-locked swap:** keep the hero in the same screen spot while its world changes.
4. **Song in another language as pure texture:** emotion without competing with the on-screen words.
5. **Cut to silence on the logo.**
