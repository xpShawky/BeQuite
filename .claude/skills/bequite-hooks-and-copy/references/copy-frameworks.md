# Copy frameworks, each with a real example (2026-11-21)

Pick the one whose real example looks most like the piece you are making. Each lists its beats with times measured in the example.

## 1. Persona story (short video, 60-90 s)
Beats: persona in a bind (0-12 s) → the two bad options (12-25 s) → turn, addressing the persona ("استنى بس يا كريم") (25 s) → the feature on screen (27-50 s) → why it is better (52-60 s) → its limit (60-73 s) → payoff in the story (73-79 s) → CTA in the persona's terms ("ولو عايز تبقى ناصح زي كريم تابعني") (79-88 s).
Example: Fathy & Abusrea, "اسأل Claude Code وهو شغال… من غير ما توقفه", 14,061 views, 6.7x (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LgvGG6GfVjI).
Use for: one feature of one tool. The stated limit is what makes it credible.

## 2. Insider take (short video, 60-90 s)
Beats: who I am and where I work (0-5 s) → the common belief → what actually happens at my job → the lesson.
Example: Yehia Tech, "شركتك بتدفعلك عشان تكتب كود بس؟", 204,989 views, 8.7x (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XQ666RUcDtQ). Keyword pop-up labels (the company's logo, "Contractor") reinforce the identity.
Use for: careers, "AI and your job" (radar brief W47 item 4).

## 3. News + receipts (short video, 2-3 min)
Beats: urgency line (0-4 s) → what happened, with the real tweet or doc on screen (split screen: source on top, face below) → what it means for you → the tool or fix → a casual close.
Example: Ahmed Nagdy, "بعد اللي حصل لـ GitLab و PHP وغيره — افحص الكود بتاعك بالسكيل دي", 35,229 views (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FLFGYGpZjes).
Use for: launches and incidents within 48 hours. Every claim has its source on screen.

## 4. Result first (short video, 60-75 s)
Beats: "look at this" (0-1 s, big type) → the result plays → how it was made → the free route → link.
Example: Mahmoud Khidr, "الفيديو ده اتعمل كله بكلود ومجاناً", 15,144 views, 5.8x (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xkf7N9kYlW8).
Use for: anything whose output is visual. The result must be good enough to stand alone.

## 5. Claim + receipt + save (X / Telegram post)
Beats: the claim in one line with a number → the proof (a link, a screenshot, a step list) → "احفظه" or a question.
Example: X posts measured in `bequite-writing-dna/references/tells-ar.md`: the top Arabic AI posts (Nov 2026) break lines (median 4 per post), use an emoji in about half, and carry a link in a quarter. One case: a post on running Claude Code for free, 116,317 views, 1,798 bookmarks.
Use for: tools, lists, resources (bookmarks show "save for later" value). Check every "free" claim at the vendor before posting.

## Captions and titles
- Title = the promise in the viewer's words ("كيفية عمل فيديو لأكثر من 30 ثانية ببلاش"), not the feature's name.
- On-screen captions: 2-4 words, one keyword in a colour (format sheet).
- Post caption: first line repeats the hook's concrete noun.
