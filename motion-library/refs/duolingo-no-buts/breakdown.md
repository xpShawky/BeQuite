## 4. Signature techniques
- **Transitions:** none; one shot.
- **Typography:** lowercase rounded brand face, two lines, in brand green, appears on the punch frame with a smoke puff.
- **Camera:** locked-off.
- **Colour and background:** pure white + brand green.
- **Pacing:** 3 s setup, 2 s payoff.
- **Sound:** −22.5 LUFS (very quiet; made for sound-off feeds, sound is a bonus), four silences in 5 s.

## 5. When to use this style
- Fits: social cut-downs, mascots, a reminder message, bumpers; 5–6 s.
- Avoid when: you need to show product function.
- Cost and difficulty: low with a rigged character (Blender/AE); the craft is the joke and the timing.

## 6. What to steal (adapt, never copy)
1. **Sound-led build into a one-frame punch** for a 6 s BeQuite bumper (a rising tone as tests run → one frame "all gates green").
2. **Text appears on the punch frame, not before.**
3. **Hold the payoff in silence for 2 s** so it reads and loops.
