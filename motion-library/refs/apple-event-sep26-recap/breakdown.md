## 5. When to use this style
- **Fits:** recaps, changelogs, "everything new in v4" films, social cuts of a long launch, 60–100 s; any brand that can afford a playful voice.
- **Avoid when:** the brand voice must stay formal; there is no long source film to scrub through.
- **Cost and difficulty:** low once the source films exist: AE/Premiere, a recreated player UI (shape layers + a hover thumbnail), a voice with personality. For BeQuite: scrub through the launch film or through a session transcript/timeline.

## 6. What to steal (adapt, never copy)
1. **UI as the constant:** keep a player bar / terminal frame fixed and change everything inside it; the UI makes fast cuts feel like one continuous action.
2. **Open on the finale, then rewind** (skip to the end, promise to come back to it).
3. **Thumbnail → screen match:** the preview image becomes the content of the next shot at the same place.
4. **End on a satisfying loop** (fold, toggle, open/close) instead of a final claim.
5. **Creator-voice VO** that reacts to the picture; duck the music for its suspense pauses.
