## 4. Signature techniques
- **Presentation:** the browser card does not move (locked-off 100 %); only the page scrolls. The 3D blob behind is static. Premium comes from the restraint.
- **Site design:** white, black type, greys; one dark section with blue light; huge marquee type at the end.
- **Sound:** none in the file (no audio track). Instagram reels usually add music in-app; it was not captured.

## 5. When to use this style
- Fits: presenting a website or app concept (Dribbble/Behance style), BeQuite-built sites.
- Avoid when: the site has no motion of its own; the card frame will not save a static page.
- Cost: low for the frame (AE or Figma: card + 3D backdrop render); the site's scroll animations are the cost.

## 6. What to steal
1. **Floating card over a soft 3D sculpture** as a presentation stage (render the blob once in Blender).
2. **Two images entering from opposite sides to meet** under the headline.
3. **End on an oversized marquee wordmark** that breaks out of the frame.
