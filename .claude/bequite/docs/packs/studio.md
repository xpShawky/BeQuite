# The studio pack

Films, decks and content. 5 commands and 10 skills, on top of core. Every studio command is reference-first and stops for the owner's yes before anything costs money or leaves the machine.

## What it does

- **`/bq-motion`** directs a film: references, script and beats, style lock, tool and render plan (each waits for a yes), animatic, build rounds, voice and sound, render and QC, recuts and launch posts. Skills: motion-director, kinetic-type (with the BQ Kinetic engine and panel for chat-directed After Effects films), sound-voice, render-pipeline.
- **`/bq-presentation`** builds PPTX or HTML decks from studied reference decks and runs the presentation tell list before delivery. Skill: presentation-builder.
- **`/bq-content`** takes one piece from what is moving in a niche to a packaged draft: radar, study, script, produce, package, queue, from-work; plus brand-kit, community and course. Skills: trend-radar, format-dna, hooks-and-copy, publish-prep, with writing-dna and human-taste from core.
- **`/bq-watch`** watches a video at the lowest cost that answers the question (metadata, captions, audio and Whisper, storyboards, only the seconds that matter, a low-resolution study), reporting the bytes each step downloaded. Skill: video-intel.
- **`/bq-dna`** measures the format of a niche's top five outliers into a one-page format sheet. Skill: format-dna.

## Needs on the machine

ffmpeg and yt-dlp for video work; After Effects, Premiere, Blender, DaVinci Resolve or Remotion depending on the render plan (`/bq-doctor` records what is there). Nothing is installed without the owner's yes.

## Boundaries

No post, upload or schedule without a yes per post. Platform apps and websites are never automated; publishing goes through official routes or the owner's own device. Third-party media studied as references stays local and never enters git.
