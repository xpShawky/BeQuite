# "Construct" promo by Tobi (made with Fable 5.2, "one prompt"): breakdown

**File:** `inbox/old/Tobi on X_ made this in 10 minutes with fable 5.2 ….mp4` · **Source:** creator post on X (claims it was made in 10 minutes from one prompt; UNVERIFIED) · **Length / format:** 18.15 s, 1920x1080, 30 fps · **Studied from:** contact sheet + 10 cuts + loudness.
**Category:** product promo · kinetic type · UI demo

## 1. The story in one line
"Anyone can ship a product now, but most stall. Every product that lasts starts with a system, and every system starts with the right skills." That is **BeQuite's own pitch**, almost word for word.

## 2. Story shape
Claim (the world changed) → **BUT** (the problem) → insight (system) → mechanism (skills) → product name. A classic problem/turn/solution in 18 s.

## 4. Signature techniques
- **Design-tool overlays** (bounding boxes, handles, cursor paths) as the visual motif: it says "made by a builder".
- **Typographic system:** grotesk sans for statements, **italic serif for the one key word** ("System"), dot-matrix for drama ("BUT", "stall").
- **Palette:** near-black + cream + one terracotta orange. Very controlled.
- **Sound:** one long riser from −31 to −19 LUFS over 16 s, then **an impact (−8) exactly on the logo cut**. Quiet overall (−19.4 integrated; peak +0.4 dB is a small overshoot).

## 5. When to use this style
- **Fits:** dev tools, AI agents, SaaS, anything sold to builders; 15–30 s; the strongest **direct template for a BeQuite film**.
- **Avoid when:** consumer lifestyle brands (too technical), or when there is no real product UI to show.
- **Cost:** low-to-medium. Entirely typographic plus simple 3D (wireframes, particles, cards): Remotion/HyperFrames, AE, or Blender for the cubes.

## 6. What to steal
1. **The "BUT" turn** in giant type: one word that flips the story.
2. **Italic serif for the single key word** in a sans-serif film.
3. **Skill cards swapping in place** to show what the product contains; BeQuite has real skills and commands to show.
4. **A riser that pays off on the logo cut.**

## 7. Watch-outs
It is close to the generic "AI startup" look (black, orange, particles). Keep the structure; give BeQuite its own visual motif (for example the work-folder tree or the gate) instead of particles.
