# Tells: what makes a kinetic-type film look machine-made

Every item here was seen in generated motion and is absent from the human references. Check your JSON against the list before sending it.

## Rhythm

- Every word lands exactly one beat after the previous one, in every scene.
- No breath before the important word, so nothing feels important.
- Every scene has the same number of words and the same length.
- Words land faster than a viewer can read: more than four landings in one second.

## Hierarchy

- All words the same size, or everything big.
- Connectors ("the", "of", "to") at full size in their own rows.
- Two or three accent colours fighting; accenting a word that is not the payoff.
- A slam in every scene, so none of them hits.

## Layout

- Full sentences dropped in as one item.
- Rows broken by width only, splitting a phrase that belongs together ("AI / harness" on two rows when "AI harness" is one idea).
- One very long row under very short ones, with no reason.
- More than five rows: it becomes a paragraph, not a block.

## Motion and camera

- `fade` on every scene (soft and generic); use cuts and whips, and fade only at the end.
- `whip-up` after every scene, including the last.
- `static` camera everywhere, so the film becomes slides.

## Words

- Generic hype: "revolutionary", "next-level", "unleash", "game-changer", "supercharge", "seamless".
- Rhetorical questions as the opening line.
- Claims with numbers the user never gave you. If a number matters, ask for it.
