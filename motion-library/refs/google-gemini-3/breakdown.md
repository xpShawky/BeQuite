## 4. Signature techniques
- **Transitions:** none in the classic sense: camera flies, blurs and rack-focus pulls carry 115 s of one continuous space; one hard cut with a whoosh at 01:21.09.
- **Typography:** Google Sans, white, mid-size, one keyword at a time, blur-in/blur-out; labels in dark glass chips.
- **Camera:** a slow virtual camera through particles; UI planes tilted in 3D with depth of field.
- **Colour:** navy-black space, white type, the Gemini gradient (blue/purple/pink/yellow) only in the sparkle and particle accents.
- **Pacing:** even; the narration drives everything (voice present 91% of runtime, 264 words).
- **Sound:** −24.4 LUFS integrated (≈10 LU below the −14 web norm), LRA 2.8 LU (very flat), true peak −8.9 dBFS: a quiet, low-dynamics master. A soft pulse bed (≈152 BPM, confidence 2.71). Many small −15 dB dips (17 "sudden drops") sit under the narration's sentence breaks.

## 5. When to use this style
- **Fits:** a feature-complete explainer for developers; a 90–120 s "what's in this release" film; docs pages.
- **Avoid when:** the goal is emotion or a hero launch; social (too slow to hook).
- **Cost and difficulty:** medium: AE or Blender 3D camera, particle field, UI captures as planes, a script locked to VO. Low creative risk, low ceiling.

## 6. What to steal (adapt, never copy)
1. **Spoken-word captions:** surface only the one keyword of each VO line, blur-in on the spoken syllable. Good for a BeQuite explainer cut.
2. **UI planes tilted in 3D space** with depth of field, instead of flat screen recordings.
3. **Word carousel** for a list of capabilities (plan, build, verify, ship).
4. **Lineage line:** a point of light travelling one line through versions (v1 → v4 "Titan").
