## 4. Signature techniques
- **Transitions:** 9 of 22 detector cuts are confirmed graphic or position-locked matches on `matches.jpg` (41 %), the highest share in the Apple set. Three families: (1) one object locked while the environment changes (plateau ×3, 10.94-13.4); (2) centred-object chains where the object itself changes (chip → cloud → chamber → heat map, 13.4-20.2); (3) the 0.30 s focal-length ladder (32.23-33.73). 11 cuts go to or from black type cards.
- **Typography:** a neutral grotesk (SF-style), white or 60 % grey, about 3-4 % of frame height, centred on black. Lines build **word by word in their final positions** (the line never re-centres): new words appear dim and brighten within ~0.1-0.3 s ("8x optical" at 29.9, the full line by 30.3, held to 31.2: 0.4 s build + 0.9 s hold). Key words carry the weight ("3x better **scratch resistance**"). The only display face is the extended orange "PRO / ALL / OUT" at the open.
- **Camera:** CG product shots with slow turns and moving light (inner-motion 59 % of running time), locked type cards (28 %); the only push-ins are the zoom ladder. Product lighting: black void, one raking key from above or the side, highlights on the chamfered edges, the orange finish as the warm accent; the line-up is revealed by a light rising over it rather than by a camera move.
- **Colour and background:** black world; orange as the single hero hue (opening, slogan, names, logo); cool blue only for the thermal map and the rain plate.
- **Pacing:** ASL by quarter 2.48 / 2.98 / **1.24** / 4.97 s: steady, a burst at the ladder in Q3, then the long packshot. Mean edit interval with the hidden cuts added: 1.70 s.

## 5. When to use this style
- Fits: hardware or "pro" software launches with a list of hard claims; 45-60 s; 16:9 hero film or a keynote opener.
- Avoid when: the product has no physical or visual "proof" per claim, or the audience needs a human story.
- Cost and difficulty: high. CG product model with good materials (Blender / Cinema 4D + Redshift / Octane), one lighting rig reused across shots, a sound designer building a low boom per transition, an editor timing type builds to the bed. The type cards and the ladder are cheap; the plateau-in-three-worlds shot needs FX sims (rain, dust, debris).

## 6. What to steal (adapt, never copy)
1. **Position-locked ladder:** one subject fixed in frame, 5-7 cuts at 0.3 s, each a step on a scale (focal length, version number, file size, agent count), last step held 1.5 s. For BeQuite: the same terminal prompt at the same spot while the task inside grows.
2. **Feature by environment swap:** lock the hero object and change only the world around it, 0.8 s per world, to prove robustness without words.
3. **A boom on every transition, not a cut on every beat:** let the sound designer place a low hit under each cut (64 % here) instead of forcing cuts onto the music grid.
4. **Word-by-word spec line in final position:** words appear dim, brighten in 0.1-0.3 s, line held ~1 s, on black, 3-4 % height.
5. **Hero-colour close:** names and logo in the one colour of the product, then 5 s of black silence.
