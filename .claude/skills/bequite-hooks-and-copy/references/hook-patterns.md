# Hook patterns measured in Arabic AI Shorts (2026-11-21)

Source: opening caption lines (`ar-orig`) of 11 recent Shorts from five channels whose videos beat their own Shorts median (2.6x-30.9x). "Formula" maps each to the closest of the 21 in youtube-agent-skill's `hooks.json`; "new" means none of the 21 fits.

| Pattern | Real opening (channel) | Formula | Why it works | Fails when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Persona in a bind, with a number | "كريم المحاسب ساب كلود كود يراجع فولدر فيه 200 فاتورة ويجمعهم كلهم في شيت واحد" (Fathy & Abusrea, 6.7x) | new | a person the viewer recognises, a concrete load, a pressure that the next line raises | the persona has no problem yet; the number is vague |
| Persona + clock | "كل يوم الساعة 9:00 الصبح، المدير بيدخل المكتب ويسأل كريم سؤال واحد" (Fathy & Abusrea) | The Clock (adapted) | a fixed daily moment = a routine the tool replaces | the clock is decoration, not the problem |
| Persona + deadline | "سلمى عندها ثلاث أبحاث لثلاث مواد وكلهم تسليمهم آخر الأسبوع" (Fathy & Abusrea) | new | stakes in one breath | too tidy: a judge flagged its parallel structure |
| First-person identity | "أنا كراجل شغال كونتراكتور مع شركة اسمها تابي" (Yehia Tech, 8.7x, 204,989 views) | The Insider | a real role at a named company = authority without a claim | the role is invented or irrelevant |
| Result first | "بص على الفيديو ده كده، في مينا قديم وقت الغروب كان فيه قطة" (Mahmoud Khidr, 5.8x) | The Reveal | the proof plays before the claim | the result is ordinary |
| News urgency | "حتى لو كنت عايش تحت صخرة أكيد سمعت عن اللي حصل إمبارح في عالم السايبر" (Ahmed Nagdy, 2.6x) | The Deadline | assumes the viewer already heard, creates fear of missing it | the news is not fresh (date it) |
| Company claim with a number | "شركة أنثروبيك اللي عاملة كلود بتقول لك إن هما النهارده بيعملوا أكتر من 30 ألف في اليوم الواحد" (Yehia Tech, 2.9x) | The Statistic | a source + a big number | the unit is missing (a judge flagged it) |
| Mid-action problem | "...الليمت بتاع كلود كود وهو في نص تاسك مهمة المفروض تسليمها كمان ساعة" (Fathy & Abusrea) | The Mistake | the pain at its worst moment | the fix is not shown fast |
| Series / tutorial label | "الجزء الثالث من سلسلة شروحات كاب كات، النهارده هشرح لك ازاي تعمل موشن تراكنج" (JORA TECH) | (none) | works for returning viewers | weak for strangers |
| Superlative headline | "اقوى اداة مجانية لكشف الكتابة بالذكاء الاصطناعي" (Mohamed Ansary) | The Superlative | a strong title card carries it | spoken alone it reads machine-made: 3 of 5 blind judges picked it as "AI" |
| Plain intro + title card | "تعالوا في هذا الفيديو نلقي الضوء على هذه الميزة الرائعة ولله الحمد ببلاش" (Mohamed Ansary, 30.9x) | (none) | the on-screen title ("كيفية عمل فيديو لأكثر من 30 ثانية / Google Omni") states the promise | without the title card, nothing is promised |

## What the eleven share

- Speech from 0.0 s; one sentence; Egyptian colloquial in 9 of 11 (one mixes formal phrases, one is formal).
- A concrete noun in 8 of 11: a name (كريم، سلمى), a company (تابي، أنثروبيك), a number (200، 9:00، 30 ألف), a scene (مينا قديم وقت الغروب). The three without one lean on a title card, a series label, or "yesterday's" news.
- No greeting, no "in this video I will" except the two that lean on a title card or a series.

## The 21 formulas as a checklist

Statistic · Someone Else's Result · The Mistake · Contrarian Flip · The Reveal · The Superlative · The Clock · I Tried It · The Question · Before and After · The Teardown · The Stack · The Warning · The List · The Receipt · The Insider · The Impossible Claim · The Comparison · The Origin · The Deadline · The Direct Address (youtube-agent-skill, MIT). Use them to name and vary a pattern, not as a source of wording. Its own scorer is honest about its limits: it separates bad hooks from real ones, but a creator's hits from their misses "barely at all".
