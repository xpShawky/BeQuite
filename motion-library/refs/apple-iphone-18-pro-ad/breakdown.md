## 4. Signature techniques
- **Transitions:** only 9 hard cuts (9.8/min), ASL mean 5.49 s; pacing by quarter [13.73, 3.43, 3.43, 3.43] s. Most changes are soft: light, smoke, blur dissolves and type builds (33 non-cut picture events). One confirmed position-locked cut at 17.59 where the centred type line is the constant.
- **Typography:** Apple system sans (SF Pro look, UNVERIFIED), sentence case, centred, ~3–4 % of frame height. Three treatments: (1) whole line in ~40 % grey, a white highlight sweeps it word by word; (2) the line builds word by word over a render; (3) the line racks in from blur or dissolves out through blur. One exception: the extruded chrome "PRO FURTHER" / "PRO" at the end.
- **Camera:** slow orbits and pushes on CG renders; macro on lens and chip; a light beam as the first move.
- **Colour and background:** black stage, white/grey type, the deep rose/burgundy finish on the camera plateau, cold blue x-ray renders; light is the main material.
- **Pacing:** one long ladder of features at ~2–2.5 s per line, no breath until the brand line.
- **Sound:** instrumental score, no VO (transcript empty). −15.1 LUFS, **LRA 6.7 LU (most dynamic of the 2026 set)**, true peak −1.5 dBFS, tempo confidence 1.09 (no steady beat), harmonic share 0.70.
- **Sync, measured:** hard cuts on strong onsets 0 % (n = 9), but **picture events (type changes, flashes, cuts) land within ±1 frame of a percussive hit 16 % of the time vs 4 % by chance (×4.3)**; ±2 frames 26 % vs 7 % (×3.6). The type is timed to the sparse accents of the score, not to a beat grid.

## 5. When to use this style
- **Fits:** spec-heavy launches, performance/feature films without a narrator, 30–60 s; dark premium brands; BeQuite's "most powerful harness" claim ladder.
- **Avoid when:** warmth or people matter more than specs; very short social cuts (each line needs ~2 s to read).
- **Cost and difficulty:** CG product renders (Blender / C4D / Unreal) or high-end macro, AE for type (word-by-word highlight is a range selector on fill colour), volumetric smoke for the end. Medium–high.

## 6. What to steal (adapt, never copy)
1. **Word-by-word light sweep:** set the line in grey, sweep white across it word by word (AE text animator, range selector by words, ease in/out, ~0.3–0.5 s per word).
2. **Type as the still point:** keep the line centred and fixed while the render underneath changes on a percussive accent.
3. **Cut-less ladder:** dissolve/blur from feature to feature; save the one hard hit for the brand line.
4. **Light-beam opener:** a single beam finding the hero object in black.
5. **One loud type moment:** 3D chrome brand line in smoke, then a quiet logo and 5 s of silence.
