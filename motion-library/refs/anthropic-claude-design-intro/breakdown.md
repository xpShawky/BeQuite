## 4. Signature techniques
- **Transitions:** 11 cuts, ASL 6.8 s, curve [5.1, 10.2, 5.1, 4.08]. Long takes in the middle, faster cuts in the export beat. Two white flashes, three motion-carried cuts.
- **Typography:** the product's own UI type; serif "Claude" wordmark only at the end.
- **Camera:** virtual camera on a recording: slides (right 13.6 %, left 6.2 %), push-ins on buttons, crops so UI text is readable.
- **Colour:** Anthropic cream/off-white UI with the coral accent (Send, Tweaks, Comment buttons) and one dark globe hero.
- **Sound:** music only (the "Thank you" in transcript.md is a Whisper hallucination on music). −13.0 LUFS (hotter than the −14 social norm), LRA 10.5, true peak +0.7 dBFS on this download. Cuts on onsets 18 % vs chance 19 %: the edit does not chase the beat; 45 % sit on the beat grid.

## 5. When to use this style
- Fits: BeQuite itself (a tool used through a UI/terminal); any agent/AI product where "it really does it" is the message; 60-90 s.
- Avoid when: the audience needs emotion before function (pair it with a mood opener).
- Cost: low-medium. Real screen recordings at high resolution, a virtual camera in AE/Screen Studio-like tools, a clean end card. The cost is preparing beautiful demo projects.

## 6. What to steal
1. **Real UI, long takes, no VO**: let the product's own text tell the story.
2. **Push-in on the button just before it is pressed.**
3. **Agent checklist ticking** (Plan / write_file / Progress 5/5) as the "work is happening" beat: BeQuite has the same (tasks.md, gates).
4. **Same menu across three projects in three 1 s cuts** to prove "everywhere".
5. **End in silence on the wordmark.**
