# Audio licences

Most important first: **check the live terms page before every commercial project, save proof (page, plan, date) in the licence ledger, and match the licence to the real use: organic posts, client work, paid ads, broadcast.** Terms change often; everything below is a snapshot from research notes dated November 2026 and is **UNVERIFIED until re-checked**. Visual AI licences (video, image, 3D) are in `.claude/skills/bequite-motion-director/references/ai-generation.md`.

## General rules

- A subscription usually licenses **you, the subscriber**. If a client will run the film as a **paid ad**, check that your plan covers both client work and paid ads.
- "Free for commercial use, no attribution" stock libraries can still trigger **YouTube Content ID claims** on some tracks: keep the licence certificate / download page to dispute a claim.
- Free tiers of many AI voice and AI music tools are **non-commercial** and may require an attribution credit; paid tiers usually include a commercial licence (beta features sometimes excluded).
- Some TTS providers require you to **tell listeners the voice is AI-generated**; platforms label realistic AI audio. Check both at M9.
- Custom / cloned voices need the speaker's consent and often an approval process. Never clone a real person's voice without written consent.
- Whether published content stays cleared after cancelling a music subscription is often not stated: ask the provider in writing and keep the answer.

## Snapshot of what research notes found (re-verify)

| Source type | What the notes said |
|---|---|
| Free stock music/SFX library (Pixabay-type) | commercial use without attribution; no standalone redistribution; some tracks still claimed by Content ID |
| Subscription music libraries | entry tiers covered personal social/web; client work, digital ads and broadcast needed higher tiers (one library: ads only on Pro/Enterprise; another: "Social" tier excluded client work; another: individual tier excluded client work) |
| AI music (free tiers) | no commercial use; attribution required on some |
| AI TTS (free tiers) | no commercial use; attribution in the title on some; all paid plans of one major provider included a commercial licence |
| TTS with disclosure rule | one provider's terms required clear disclosure that the voice is AI |
