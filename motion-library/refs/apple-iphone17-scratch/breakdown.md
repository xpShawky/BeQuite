## 5. When to use this style
- Fits: a single durability, reliability or safety claim; 20-40 s; social and pre-roll where a story beats a spec list.
- Avoid when: the product has many claims to make, or the claim cannot be dramatised as one physical action.
- Cost and difficulty: medium: one location, a small cast, one practical stunt (a sliding phone on a long table). The in-scene end card is cheap.

## 6. What to steal (adapt, never copy)
1. **Proof by story:** one scene where the product survives a social power move. For BeQuite: a code review where someone slams a "this will break" comment, and every gate is already green.
2. **Quiet before the slam:** let the bed fall to near silence for 5-6 s, then give the loudest hit to the product's action.
3. **End card inside the scene:** claim and punchline over the real set, the logo sitting above the product.
4. **Hard cut to black and silence** right after the punchline.
