## 4. Signature techniques
- **Transitions:** word swaps on a fixed baseline (0–1.6 s), a growing line as a transition (6–10 s), 12 graphic-match cuts inside the tool demos (same object, new persona), a pull-out from screen content to device (22.5 s).
- **Typography:** two families: a heavy editorial serif (YOUR / SUBSCRIPTION) and a flared display face for Craft / Free / Forever, shown as an editable object with handles. Brand acid green (#9EF06A-like, estimate) on charcoal.
- **Camera:** mostly 2D inner motion (74 %); one 3D move: the laptop pull-out.
- **Colour and background:** charcoal + acid green + white, with the artists' saturated colours only in the 1-second burst.
- **Pacing:** ASL 0.91 s, median 0.37 s; curve [3.76, 0.94, 0.30, 7.52]: long problem act, fast demos, fastest burst, then a long hold on the product. Classic accelerate → stop.
- **Sound:** −9.1 LUFS (showreel master, too hot for platforms), LRA 4.0 LU, ≈ 76 BPM. **Bass is used as structure**: no bass during the problem (1.0–10.4 s) and the "free" joke (18.5–20.5 s), bass in for the reveals. Female VO (F0 ≈ 194 Hz), 111 wpm, conversational, under nearly the whole film (91 %); 31 of 32 cuts land mid-line.

## 5. When to use this style
- Fits: creative software, pricing/positioning changes, manifesto launches, 15–45 s, social + web.
- Avoid when: the brand is calm/enterprise; when there is no real UI worth showing.
- Cost and difficulty: medium-high. AE for kinetic type and UI recreation, real app captures for the edits, a 3D laptop (C4D/Blender), 12 commissioned illustrations for the burst. A strong VO read and a track with editable bass stems.

## 6. What to steal (adapt, never copy)
1. **Persistent UI chip as anchor:** for BeQuite, the command pill (`/bq-auto`) or the gate bar stays in the same place while the content changes under it.
2. **Bass as structure:** no low end during the pain, bass drops in on the product word.
3. **Type as an editable object:** show the key word with selection handles, then transform it.
4. **Clutter build of legacy dialogs** as the "before" (for BeQuite: a pile of half-finished chats, broken builds, "it works on my machine").
5. **Pull-out screen reveal:** the abstract artwork turns out to be on the product's screen.
