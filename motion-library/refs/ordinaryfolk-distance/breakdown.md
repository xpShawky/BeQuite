## 1. The story in one line
Samuel Morse, a painter, rides home for six days to his sick wife and learns she was already buried; he spends the rest of his life inventing the telegraph so no one feels that distance again. It feels intimate and devastating.

## 2. Story shape
Who he was (painter) → the call away → the message → the race home (accelerating) → the reveal (stop) → what he did with the grief (telegraph) → dots and lines become a title. Classic three-act with a delayed reveal and a "this is why" coda.

## 4. Signature techniques
- **Transitions:** cut-paper and ink frames, hand-made boil; no digital transitions. Shot detection reports 164 "cuts" because many are animation drawings (median 0.21 s).
- **Typography:** none until the end: Morse dashes become letters, then small spaced caps credits.
- **Camera:** none; flat artwork on paper (top-down rostrum feel).
- **Colour and background:** black ink, grey wash, white paper; textures of brush and paper grain. No colour at all.
- **Pacing:** curve [1.07, 2.34, 0.55, 17.18]: exposition slows, the ride rushes, the reveal stops, the coda holds.
- **Sound:** VO-led, −16.3 LUFS, LRA 3.8 LU; the narration measures 190 wpm over 72 % of the film (fast for narration, delivered as a storyteller's run-on sentences); cuts are not on onsets (lift ×1.0). Music is a steady bed that never swells; it thins at the end.

## 5. When to use this style
- Fits: origin stories, founder stories, "why we built this" films, podcasts turned into films; 2–4 min.
- Avoid when: you need to show a product; short social ads.
- Cost and difficulty: high in craft hours (painting and cutting every frame; ≈ 5 drawings per second in the montage), low in tools (paper, ink, a camera on a stand, or a digital imitation with real scanned textures). A strong written script is the core.

## 6. What to steal (adapt, never copy)
1. **Seed the ending motif in the first frame** (for BeQuite: a cursor dash and a dot that later become the logo or a gate tick).
2. **Accelerate, then stop dead on the reveal:** 0.2 s frames → a 15 s hold.
3. **The form is the invention:** the film's shapes are the product's language (Morse dots and dashes); for BeQuite, gates and checkboxes.
4. **Hand-made texture as anti-AI signal:** scanned ink and paper grain make a film read as human.
5. **"Why it exists" coda** after the story.
