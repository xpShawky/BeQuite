## 1. The story in one line
"All progress has a starting point": one dot divides and becomes every human leap, from the first tools to the moon, until it becomes ChatGPT's own voice. Feels inevitable and optimistic.

## 2. Story shape
One dot → it divides (life) → history told in dots (tools, wheel, horse, agriculture, ships, steam, light, X-ray, flight, DNA, TV, moon, computer, chip) → dots become the product's voice indicator → users' voices → tagline → product orb → wordmark → logo. A single guide element from first frame to last.

## 4. Signature techniques
- **Transitions:** morphs inside one dot system carry almost the whole film (inner motion 55.8% of runtime); 37 detected "cuts" are mostly flash frames and polarity flips (7 white-flash, 8 black). Cuts on strong onsets only 3% vs 14% chance (lift ×0.19): the picture deliberately does not hit the beat; changes happen between hits.
- **Typography:** one tiny centred sans line, one typed wordmark. The dots are the "type".
- **Camera:** none; flat 2D frame with slow push-ins on halftone footage.
- **Colour:** strict black and white for 54 s; the first colour (blue orb) is the product.
- **Pacing:** [3.76, 3.01, 0.56, 3.01] s per quarter: long morphs, one burst, long close.
- **Sound:** music only until 48 s; −17.4 LUFS, LRA 5.8 LU, true peak −0.4 dBFS; the steadiest beat in this set (confidence 5.22). Voice is only 11% of runtime: five user voices in ≈6 s (≈300 wpm effective because they overlap), then the product's voice once.

## 5. When to use this style
- **Fits:** brand films for abstract products, "where we come from / where we go" stories, 30–60 s; very strong in 1:1 and 9:16 too (flat 2D, centred).
- **Avoid when:** you need to show a real UI workflow, or you have no simple primitive in your product's visual language.
- **Cost and difficulty:** medium. AE with a halftone/dot effect over footage (or particles with target shapes), plus hand-built morphs. No AI needed. Risk: the morphs need real animation craft to feel smooth.

## 6. What to steal (adapt, never copy)
1. **One primitive carries the whole history.** For BeQuite: the terminal cursor block, a checkmark, or a gate square that becomes every stage of building software, then becomes the BeQuite UI itself.
2. **No colour until the product.** Monochrome for the story; the brand colour appears only on the product reveal.
3. **Users' voices instead of a narrator:** five quick real requests ("plan this", "verify that", in several languages, Arabic included).
4. **Type the wordmark** like a command, letter by letter.
5. **Don't cut on the beat during morphs;** let transformations breathe between hits.
