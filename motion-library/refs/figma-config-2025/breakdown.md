## 4. Signature techniques
- **Transitions:** 100 cuts; 21 tagged motion-carried (a cursor or panel moving continues across the cut), 9 cuts to black (chapter cards), 9 same-setup (stop-motion states of one artwork). Chapter cards on black are the act breaks.
- **Typography:** chapter titles in Figma's sans, white on black, centred, each with a unique treatment that is the feature itself (grid split for Sites, "Thinking…" for Make, brush circle for Draw, nested frames for Buzz). Demo content uses many display faces because it is "the user's" work.
- **Camera:** screen-space only. Moves are UI moves (scroll, pan, zoom on canvas). No 3D devices.
- **Colour and background:** one shared demo universe (nature/ecology: Chesapeake Bay, Crater Lake National Park, Hartford Botanical, Nature Fest) gives four products one look. Black for chapter cards; saturated content colour inside.
- **Pacing:** ASL 0.80 s, median 0.5 s, 73.9 cuts/min. Curve [0.68, 1.02, 0.73, 0.78]: almost flat; the chapter cards (≈ 1–1.5 s holds) are the only breaths.
- **Sound:** music only, −15.7 LUFS, LRA 7.0 LU. A 9 s sparse intro (clicks, no bass) before the beat, 129 BPM steady to 75.5 s, then a fall to silence under the logo. Sync lift ×1.13 only: cuts follow the UI actions, not every beat.

## 5. When to use this style
- Fits: multi-feature launches, release recaps, changelog films, conference keynotes; 45–90 s; designers and developers who read UI fast.
- Avoid when: the product needs explaining (nothing is explained), mass audiences, phones (UI at 0.5 s is unreadable at small size), one-feature launches.
- Cost and difficulty: medium-high. Needs real UI states captured or rebuilt in many versions, one coherent demo dataset, a fast editor. Tools: screen capture or Figma/HTML rebuild + AE/Premiere.

## 6. What to steal (adapt, never copy)
1. **Chapter card = the feature itself.** For BeQuite: "/bq-plan" title written as a plan checklist, "/bq-verify" title with a green tick gate, "/bq-motion" title that animates.
2. **One demo universe for every chapter.** One fictional project (e.g. a small café app) goes through every BeQuite command, so the film has continuity.
3. **Sparse intro, beat on the first wow.** Hold the bass until the first real transformation.
4. **"Thinking…" as a beat.** Show the agent's wait state as a designed moment.
5. **Summary line before the logo** ("All in Figma" → "All in one harness" type line), then logo in silence.
