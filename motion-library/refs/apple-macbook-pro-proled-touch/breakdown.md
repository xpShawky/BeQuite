## 1. The story in one line
"If you give a Mac an OLED…": a children's-book chain of consequences, one feature leading to the next, flown through on drone one-takes. Witty, effortless, inevitable.

## 4. Signature techniques
- **Transitions:** 52 hard cuts (26.7/min), ASL mean 2.2 s, median 1.13 s; pacing by quarter [9.73, 0.81, 2.92, 4.17] s: a 22.9 s one-take, then a very dense middle (the M5 flicker), then slower lifestyle and a 13.7 s one-take to end. Motion-carried cuts 9; confirmed matches: 1:14.16 (wireframe → render), 0:46.95 (pinch), 1:50.26 (lid → logo). Matches.jpg pairs at 0:59.09, 1:11.36, 1:28.02, 1:30.81 show identical pictures: detector false positives inside a shot.
- **Typography:** small white in-scene labels only ("unified memory", "3 pounds light", "Headphone jack"); no title cards until the logo.
- **Camera:** FPV drone one-takes (start and end), push-ins dominate (62 % of running time), handheld lateral moves.
- **Colour and background:** warm practical locations (a dark wood library, a daylight atrium, autumn park, sunset cliff); the screen is the brightest colour in every frame.
- **Pacing:** long → dense → medium → long; the densest moment (2–3-frame flicker) lands on the chip names.
- **Sound:** −16.0 LUFS, LRA 4.2 LU, true peak −0.8 dBFS, 143.6 BPM (low band confidence 3.37), harmonic share 0.70, silences at 28.7 and 39.8 (short breaths), 5 s silent tail. VO present 84 % of the film; 48 of 52 cuts fall mid-line.
- **Sync, measured:** cuts within ±70 ms of a strong onset 12 % (chance 15 %, ×0.78); on any onset ±2 frames 42 % (chance 38 %); on a low boom ±1 frame 15 % (chance 9 %, ×1.8). Cuts are **not** on a grid (vector strength at the beat 0.15, random 0.14). The edit follows the voice (one cut per verb or noun), and the sound designer places booms on about a quarter of the cuts.

## 5. When to use this style
- **Fits:** a product with many linked features; witty brands; 60–120 s films; launches where "everything connects" is the message (for BeQuite: research → plan → gates → evidence → release, as a chain).
- **Avoid when:** a single hero feature must dominate; serious/clinical tone.
- **Cost and difficulty:** high for real FPV over sets; in 3D the same feel comes from a continuous camera path through a modelled space (Blender) or AI plates stitched in motion blur. The script is the cheap, strong part.

## 6. What to steal (adapt, never copy)
1. **Circular causal-chain script** ("if you give X a Y, it will want a Z…"), last line returns to the first.
2. **Cut per spoken verb** in a list (swipe / drag / pinch / scroll / spin = 5 cuts).
3. **Position-locked flicker montage:** keep the product (or the BeQuite terminal) at the same spot, swap the world around it every 2–3 frames.
4. **Hidden cut in a whip:** fly from one world into another through motion blur.
5. **Product logo → brand logo at the same position** for the end card.
