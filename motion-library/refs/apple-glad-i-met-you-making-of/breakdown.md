## 5. When to use this style
- Fits: a companion to any hero film; tool brands (the BTS proves the tool did the work); 2–4 min.
- Avoid when: there is no real craft story to show.
- Cost and difficulty: low to medium if planned during the shoot (a second camera on set, interviews, a host).
