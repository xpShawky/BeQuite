## 4. Signature techniques
- **Transitions:** 10 hard cuts (11.2/min), ASL mean 4.86 s (median 1.13 s); pacing by quarter [2.23, 2.23, 13.37, 13.37] s: a cut section, then a mask-animation section. Inside the mosaic the transitions are mask moves (polygon wipes), not cuts.
- **Typography:** condensed, heavy, all-caps grotesque with a metallic extruded face, laid in 3D perspective, tilted 45°, or vertical along a mask edge; small "UP TO" / "OF" stacked as kickers next to huge numbers. The end title is flat and stacked. Typeface UNVERIFIED.
- **Camera:** mostly locked or slow inside the plates; one whip (7.73); the "camera move" is the mask motion on a black stage.
- **Colour and background:** pure black stage, warm filmic plates (workshops, kitchens, studios), silver product, white/silver type. No brand colour.
- **Pacing:** cut section 0.7–2.3 s per shot, then 20 s of continuous geometric motion, then the product bookend.
- **Sound:** a vocal song (lyrics, not VO: the 192 wpm reading in director.md is the singer). −16.3 LUFS, LRA 4.2 LU, true peak −6.4 dBFS (the quietest peaks of the set), harmonic share 0.60.
- **Sync, measured (strongest in the 2026 set):** cuts within ±70 ms of a strong onset **50 % (chance 12 %, ×4.08)**; within ±1 frame of a percussive hit **30 % (chance 4 %, ×7.55)**; ±1 frame of any onset 40 % (chance 18 %). Here the hard cuts really are on the drums. Non-cut mask events are near chance on percussion (0 % at ±1 frame): the mosaic moves on its own regular spacing.

## 5. When to use this style
- **Fits:** pro tools, creative software, a harness for builders (BeQuite), editorial launches, 30–60 s; when you have many short real-use clips and two or three big numbers.
- **Avoid when:** the product needs a UI walkthrough, or the brand is soft and friendly.
- **Cost and difficulty:** medium. AE: shape-layer masks with track mattes, mask paths keyframed on the beat, 3D text layers (or Cinema 4D / Blender extrusions) aligned to mask edges; plates from shoots, stock, screen captures or AI video.

## 6. What to steal (adapt, never copy)
1. **Polygon windows on black:** derive the mask shapes from the product's own geometry (for BeQuite: the terminal window, the gate chevron), move them on a regular beat spacing.
2. **Type rides the mask edge:** set the big number along the edge it belongs to (tilted, vertical), small kicker words stacked beside it.
3. **Cut on the drums when you cut:** ±1 frame on the percussive hit, and let the mosaic section breathe without cuts.
4. **Bookend the product top-down**, then build the title line by line.
5. Keep true peak headroom (−6.4 dBFS here) and fade the music under the logo.
