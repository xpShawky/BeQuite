Each studied film has `refs/<slug>/breakdown.md` (story, beat sheet with timestamps, picture × sound × voice, when to use, what to steal). ## Avoid list

- Crushed, clipped masters (−6 LUFS, peaks above 0 dB); several official uploads clip: deliver −14 LUFS / −1 dBTP with real quiet moments.
- The default "AI startup" look: black + one neon accent + particles everywhere.
- Hitting every cut with a boom, or forcing cuts onto the beat grid (Apple doesn't; see TECHNIQUES "Measured truths").
- Fast cutting under a talking line; a hype-speed narrator.
- AI action carried across angles (Hell Grind: 8 of 92 cuts); holding fast AI motion; AI tells (morphing hands, gibberish text, floaty physics, clip-end slowdowns, camera moves that stop dead, reaction zooms, objects that change between shots, the golden-hour + shallow-focus + slow-motion stock look, frame-rate drops, added music). QC gate: `bequite-render-pipeline/references/qc-checklist.md` B2.
