## 5. When to use this style
- Fits: products whose virtue is lightness, simplicity or calm (a "lite" tier, a minimal app, a slim device); 30-55 s.
- Avoid when: the claim is power or drama (17 Pro's black world does that), or the product is not visually distinctive in silhouette.
- Cost and difficulty: medium. One clean CG model, a white cyclorama render setup, a hand plate for the fingertips (filmed or CG), type in AE. Easier to light than black-world films because there are no rim-light tricks.

## 6. What to steal (adapt, never copy)
1. **Show the one dimension that matters:** here the edge, held between two fingers, at the start and the end. For BeQuite ("lightweight"): the whole pack shown as a thin sliver, a single markdown folder.
2. **Fixed UI anchor across cuts:** keep one interface element (a chip, a prompt, a status pill) at the same spot while the content behind it changes every 0.7-0.8 s.
3. **White flash as the cut** in a white world; save black for the very end.
4. **One coloured word per card** in otherwise grey type.
