## 4. Signature techniques
- **Transitions:** straight cuts only; cuts avoid onsets (lift ×0.82). The repeated insert does the work.
- **Typography:** none in the body.
- **Camera:** locked inserts of the product; slow push-ins on faces (23 %); a top-down open; a crane wide.
- **Colour and background:** warm tungsten restaurant, dark; cold blue-white for the product end, a temperature switch from story to product.
- **Pacing:** ASL 1.76 s; curve [1.5, 0.94, 1.5, 3.74]: tightens during the spin, opens after the pick.
- **Sound:** music + SFX, −12.3 LUFS, **LRA 17.1 LU** (very dynamic for an ad: real silences), tempo 103 BPM with a strong beat; 6 silences ≥ 0.25 s. The game is told by stopping the beat.

## 5. When to use this style
- Fits: a hardware or UI feature that can play a game or do one visible trick; social 15–30 s.
- Avoid when: the feature needs explanation, or the product has no visible "face".
- Cost and difficulty: medium: one location, 4–5 actors, a practical or composited matrix display, a 3D product end.
