## 4. Signature techniques
- **Transitions:** 6 cuts; ASL 1.59 s, curve [0.93, 0.93, 0.93, 2.78]: fast details, long hero at the end. One position-locked graphic match (lens column held in place, 02.94).
- **Camera:** short slides and pull-outs; shallow depth of field in macros.
- **Light/material:** brushed aluminium, glass with orange internal glow (an emissive/backlight placed inside the barrel), clean white/grey studio.
- **Sound:** −14.1 LUFS, LRA 1.2; cuts on strong onsets 33 % vs chance 16% (**lift ×2.06**, the best sync in this batch): cuts are on the music hits.

## 5. When to use this style
- Fits: hardware, precision products, any "inside the machine" moment; 8-15 s vertical.
- Avoid when: no physical object; exploded views need a believable internal model.
- Cost: medium-high. Blender (Cycles): a modelled lens with separate elements, keyframed explode, an emissive light inside, area-light studio. 3-5 days.

## 6. What to steal
1. **Exploded view that assembles** (elements fall in one by one, light between them).
2. **Inset-window reveal**: a rectangle opens over one shot and holds the next.
3. **A single warm accent light inside a cool object** as the brand colour.
4. **Cut on the music hits** (here ×2 above chance).
5. For BeQuite: an exploded view of the pack (commands, skills, gates, memory) stacking into one object.
