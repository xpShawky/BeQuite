## 4. Signature techniques
- **Transitions:** 6 cuts, 5 graphic-match (4 position-locked): each section change keeps a centred element (dot, logo, phone) in place. Inside shots, motion is continuous (inner-motion 89 %).
- **Typography:** thin geometric sans, purple accent word, letters spread with crop marks ("S im ple"), a typing caret on the CTA.
- **Camera:** simulated push-ins on monitors and phones; plates are filmed/stock or AI (UNVERIFIED).
- **Colour:** white→lavender→magenta gradient frame; purple brand accent.
- **Pacing:** ASL 6.36 s, curve [11.12, 3.71, 2.78, 5.56]: one long intro/problem take, faster use cases, slower CTA.
- **Sound:** −14.1 LUFS, 112 BPM (confidence 2.64). VO 196 wpm (hype range), median pause 0.40 s. Cuts sit mid-line 4 of 6 times; cuts on onsets 17 % vs chance 18 % (no sync). The voice drives; music is a bed.

## 5. When to use this style
- Fits: SaaS / AI app explainers, 30-60 s, teaching posts ("here's the structure").
- Avoid when: the brand needs a premium, quiet feel; 196 wpm is fast and salesy.
- Cost: medium. AE for type and UI, stock or generated plates, phone/monitor mockups, TTS or VO.

## 6. What to steal
1. **The section tab bar** as an on-screen chapter indicator: good for a BeQuite "how it works" film (Research → Plan → Build → Verify).
2. **Shape → logo graphic match** at the problem→solution turn.
3. **Each use case = one real plate + one UI overlay**, separated by a single kinetic line.
4. **Typed CTA with a caret**: fits a terminal product.
