## 4. Signature techniques
- **The site design:** near-black ground, one acid-lime accent, light-ribbon WebGL/video background, editorial italic serif on one word per headline, small UI cards as illustrations, a 3D bar chart.
- **The video:** no editing at all (1 shot, inner motion only). The value is the page, not the film.
- **Sound:** −14.1 LUFS, 123 BPM (confidence 2.96), harmonic share 0.82; a library beat; hits on a ~0.65 s grid in the second half. No voice.
- **The lesson (from the wishlist note):** the caption credits giving the model a real reference to build from rather than "make it premium".

## 5. When to use this style
- Fits: showing a website/app BeQuite built; dev-audience social proof; 15-30 s.
- Avoid when: it must feel like a film; a raw scroll is a demo, not an ad.
- Cost: very low for the video (screen record + 9:16 template). The site is the cost.

## 6. What to steal
1. **Italic serif accent word** inside a sans headline ("run *themselves*").
2. **One acid accent on near-black**, used only for the single action.
3. **Burned-in claim + raw proof**: for BeQuite social posts, "BeQuite built this in one run" over an unedited scroll.
4. For a real film, re-shoot the same site with a 3D camera on the page (see ig-mitkow-3d-robotics-site) rather than a flat scroll.
