## 4. Signature techniques
- **Transitions:** 31 cuts; the detector tags 28 graphic-match, 24 position-locked and 30 "white-flash" because each cut inverts the frame. In fact every cut is a hard colour flip with the text in place.
- **Typography:** one geometric heavy sans (Gilroy/Montserrat-like, UNVERIFIED) in blue #1010D0-ish or white, plus one script italic for "own". Size encodes emphasis: function words small, key words 2-3× larger.
- **Pacing:** ASL 0.40 s, curve [0.79, 0.45, 0.32, 0.23]: steady acceleration, 147 cuts/min overall.
- **Sound:** −14.1 LUFS, harmonic share 0.77 (music bed). Cuts on strong onsets **0 %** vs 13 % chance; on the beat grid 16 %. The cuts follow the reading rhythm of the words, not the music.

## 5. When to use this style
- Fits: manifesto lines, 8-15 s reels, captions without VO, a brand with one strong colour.
- Avoid when: the brand is premium/quiet; the strobe ending can be uncomfortable (photosensitivity: flips at ~5 Hz).
- Cost: very low. AE or code: one comp per word, a colour toggle, hold keyframes. Hours.

## 6. What to steal
1. **Two-colour inversion per word** as the "cut".
2. **Size = emphasis**: small lead words stacked over one huge key word.
3. **One typeface swap** for the single most personal word.
4. **Accelerate the word cadence** (0.8 → 0.2 s) and finish on a strobe or a hard stop.
