# Ultimate Guide to Scene Transitions (StudioBinder, The Shot List ep. 9): lesson

**File:** `inbox/New/Ultimate Guide to Scene Transitions – Every Editing Transition Explained [The Shot List, Ep 9].mp4` · **Source:** StudioBinder, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TKXBAaQB03U · **Length:** ~19:29 · **Studied from:** transcript + frame check (`frames/check/examples.jpg`).
**Category:** craft lesson (transition catalogue with function for each)

## What it teaches
Nine transitions, each with one job: the cut (default), fade (open, close, chapter, breath), dissolve (time, memory, comparison), match cut (connection, time jump), iris (focus, then scale), wipe (vintage or ironic), passing/pass-by (hide the cut, keep momentum), whip pan (energy, momentum), smash cut (shock by contrast), and the sound transitions J-cut and L-cut. The point is choosing by function, not by taste.

## How each lesson applies to a 30–60 s launch film
- **Hook (#1, #12):** a break hook ("something falls or breaks") should be cut jaggedly: three to five very short angles of the same break, then a smash to stillness or silence. The contrast is the hook, not the break alone.
- **Breath (#3):** one dip to black between "the problem" and "the answer" acts as the chapter break. Use it once.
- **Match cuts (#6, #7):** the product's own shapes (a terminal cursor, a gate icon, a checkmark, the BeQuite mark) are the match vocabulary. A match dissolve is the softer tool for the calm brand moment; the hard match for energy.
- **Iris (#8):** the iris idea without the vintage look: a circular mask from a status dot opens into the full harness UI ("the world opens").
- **Pass-by and whip (#10, #11):** in 3D/UI films the "object passing the lens" is a card, a panel edge or a ribbon; pair each with a whoosh. Pick one direction for the film.
- **J/L (#13, #14):** hear the keyboard or the "gate passed" chime before the UI appears (J); let the VO line or a sound tail continue over the next plate (L) to make sound A + image B say something new.

## What to try in the lab
1. **Jagged break:** a glass panel (Blender or AE) shatters; cut it as 4 shots of 4–8 frames from angles at least 30° apart, each with its own crack layer, then a smash to a black frame with room tone. Compare to the same break as one continuous shot.
2. **Status-dot iris:** AE circular mask from a 12 px dot to full frame in 14–18 frames, strong ease-out, a soft sub-hit at the landing.
3. **J-cut timing sweep:** pre-lap the next shot's sound by 4, 8 and 16 frames; note which reads as anticipation and which as a mistake.
