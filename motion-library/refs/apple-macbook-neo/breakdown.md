## 1. The story in one line
Many colours, one simple laptop, passed from hand to hand: friendly, playful, cheap-but-premium ("Amazing Mac. Surprising price.").

## 2. Story shape
Colour shuffle (hook) → hello → hands passing it around → colour shuffle again → name → price promise → logo. No voice-over, no problem/solution: a **song-driven product dance** where the colours are the plot.

## 4. Signature techniques
- **Transitions:** practical swaps by hand (no cut), cuts on an empty white frame, three position-locked colour match cuts at 08.98 / 09.61 / 10.57, one motion-carried cut (23.85). Only 10 hard cuts in 35 s (17.1/min); ASL mean 3.19 s, median 0.96 s; pacing by quarter [4.39, 1.25, 2.93, 4.39] s: the cuts bunch in the second quarter (the colour matches) and the rest is in-camera motion.
- **Typography:** one bold extended black sans on white, centred, two cards only (name, then two-line promise). Typeface UNVERIFIED (it does not read as SF Pro).
- **Camera:** locked top-down and locked side-on; all motion is hands and product. Camera labels: inner-motion 66.9 %, move-up 28.2 % (the hand-passing take).
- **Colour and background:** pure white seamless, the five finishes are the only colour; the logo picks them up at the end.
- **Pacing:** shuffle (fast swaps) → slow greeting → three 0.6–1.0 s match cuts → long hand take → shuffle → name → logo.
- **Sound:** a vocal song carries the film (no VO; transcript lines are lyrics, not narration). −16.1 LUFS integrated, LRA 5.3 LU, true peak −0.7 dBFS, 172.3 BPM, harmonic share 0.71, music ends 29.49 and 5.5 s of silence follows.
- **Sync, measured:** cuts within ±70 ms of a strong onset 20 % (chance 14 %, lift ×1.39); on the fixed beat grid 20 %. At ±2 frames (83 ms) the picture is clearly on the music: **all picture events (cuts + hand swaps) land within ±2 frames of an onset 71 % of the time (chance 37 %, lift ×1.95)**. They do **not** land on the kick (percussive hits ±1 frame: 0–5 %, below chance): the cuts at 08.23, 08.98, 09.61, 13.95 fall **between kicks** (on the off-beat / vocal onsets), 10.57 on a kick.

## 5. When to use this style
- **Fits:** a product with colourways or variants, consumer launches, "accessible price" messages, 15–40 s social films, any brand that wants warmth without a voice.
- **Avoid when:** the product needs explaining (no VO, no UI walkthrough); dark or technical brands.
- **Cost and difficulty:** a white cyclorama, a locked top-down rig, physical products (or perfectly matched 3D renders), hand talent, and a licensed song. Low post cost; the work is rehearsal and frame-accurate placement. In 3D/AE: one locked camera, swap the object or its material on chosen frames.

## 6. What to steal (adapt, never copy)
1. **In-camera shuffle:** slide one variant over the last at the same spot, one swap per beat. For BeQuite: terminal windows / command cards sliding over each other in the same frame position, one per beat.
2. **Position-locked variant cut:** same framing, same object, only colour or content changes (3 cuts, 0.6–1.0 s each). For BeQuite: the same `/bq-` prompt line with different commands, cut on onsets within ±2 frames.
3. **Cut on the empty frame:** let the object leave, cut on the clean background, bring the next thing in.
4. **The hand wipe reveals the type:** product slides out, the name sits in the space it left.
5. **Logo cycles the product colours** (3 colours × ~0.2 s), then 5 s of silence.
