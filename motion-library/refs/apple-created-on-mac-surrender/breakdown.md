## 5. When to use this style
- Fits: "made with" proof films, creator-tool brands, live demos; 20-60 s; any product where a real person's reaction is the proof.
- Avoid when: the product's work is invisible or slow; when you do not have a real performer or user.
- Cost and difficulty: medium: one location, multi-camera coverage of a real session, a music edit. The difficulty is getting a genuine moment.

## 6. What to steal (adapt, never copy)
1. **Cut on each new layer:** introduce every component (agent, skill, gate) with a cut timed to its own sound entering the mix.
2. **Let the user's spoken line cue the next shot** ("let's bring some strings in" → strings). For BeQuite: the user types a command, the next shot is what that command summons.
3. **Silence before the first note**, then start picture and music together.
4. **End on the reaction**, then a small brand card under the music's tail.
