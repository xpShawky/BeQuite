# How Does an Editor Think and Feel? (Every Frame a Painting): lesson

**File:** `inbox/New/How Does an Editor Think and Feel_.mp4` · **Source:** Every Frame a Painting (Tony Zhou, Taylor Ramos), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3Q3eITC01Fg · **Length:** ~9:25 · **Studied from:** transcript + frame checks (`frames/check/moments.jpg`, `speakers.jpg`; film titles taken from on-screen captions where shown).
**Category:** craft lesson (timing, emotion, rhythm)

## What it teaches
When to cut is felt, not calculated, but the feeling can be trained: watch the eyes, give emotions time, build up and come down with shot length, find the natural rhythm of each shot, and break the invisible rhythm on purpose when you want discomfort or agitation.

## How each lesson applies to a 30–60 s launch film
- **#2 (eyes):** a software film has "eyes" too: the moment a state changes (a cursor stops, a plan line lights, a gate turns green). That frame is the shot. Cut from it to its consequence.
- **#3, #6 (time):** a launch film is short, so the risk is the Ant-Man failure: the key claim flashed for 30 frames is not believed. Give the hero claim (and the logo) at least 2–3 s of clean hold, with nothing else moving except a slow drift.
- **#5 (curve):** the descent after the peak should be longer than the build per shot. In a 45 s film: build over ~10–15 s with shots shrinking from ~1.5 s to ~0.3 s; the shot after the peak is the longest in the film so far (3–5 s). The 2× rule cannot be kept literally at this length; keep the shape.
- **#7 (rhythm):** each UI motion has its own rhythm (typing, a list filling, a progress bar). Cut when the motion "asks" (at the end of a phrase of typing, at the moment the bar fills), not on an arbitrary grid.
- **#9 (holding too long):** one deliberately over-held beat (an empty terminal, a blinking cursor) before the turn creates tension a fast film cannot get otherwise.

## What to try in the lab
1. **Same shot, three lengths:** a gate-passing UI shot cut at 0.8 s, 2.0 s and 3.5 s. Show the owner blind; note which length reads as "earned".
2. **Build/release test:** a 20 s sequence with shot lengths 1.5, 1.1, 0.8, 0.5, 0.3 (peak), then 1.2, 2.5, 4.0. Compare against a version with a symmetric curve.
3. **Over-hold:** insert one 1.5 s hold before the turn; check if it reads as tension or as a mistake.
