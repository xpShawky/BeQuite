# Route families (load on demand)

Where to look first, by family. Names are starting points for the live check, not recommendations; every one is verified for the user's country in the run. Last reviewed 2026-11-21.

| Family | Start with | Check first | Track fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Global freelance marketplaces | Upwork, Fiverr, Freelancer.com, PeoplePerHour, Contra | Payout methods for the country; fee (Upwork 0-15% per contract, read 2026-11-21) | skilled, long-term-stable, remote-global |
| Arab freelance platforms | Mostaql, Khamsat (both Hsoub) | Bank withdrawal country list (Egypt listed on both, read 2026-11-21); 14-working-day hold | ai-assisted, local-only, beginner |
| Creator programmes | YouTube Partner Program, TikTok Creator Rewards, Facebook and Instagram monetisation, Telegram channel ads and Stars | The programme's own availability list for the country | long-term-stable |
| Digital products | Gumroad, Payhip, a plugin or skill repo with a paid service around it | Payout method for the country; merchant-of-record and fees | long-term-stable, ai-assisted |
| AI training and data work | Platforms that hire raters, annotators and domain experts | Country eligibility (often restricted), ID checks, payout method | ai-assisted, skilled |
| Research and testing panels | Paid research and usability-testing panels | Country eligibility (many restrict to some countries) | easiest-start, no-calls |
| Translation and localisation | Agencies and marketplaces by language pair | Test requirements, per-word rates stated by the platform | skilled, remote-global |
| Local services with AI | Booking, follow-up and content automation for clinics, salons, shops; social media management | Local payment rails (bank transfer, wallets); the client's own consent rules | local-only, ai-assisted |

## Red flags to search for every candidate

- Asks for money before you earn (registration fee, starter kit, "training" you must buy).
- Pays by cheque you must deposit and partly send back.
- Asks you to reship goods or receive money for others.
- "Featured profile" or "recognition" offers that end with a payment request.
- Payout complaints dated in the last 90 days.
