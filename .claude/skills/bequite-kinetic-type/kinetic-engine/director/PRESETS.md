# The three looks

The engine owns the look; you choose a preset. Sizes are font sizes in pixels on a 1920 x 1080 frame; the camera scales the whole block, so they set proportions, not the final size on screen.

## night-gold (default; the BeQuite brand)

- Pure black background. Text near-white (#F4F4F6); accent gold (#F2B53A).
- Font: Google Sans Flex, Bold for M, L, XL and SemiBold for S. Rounded, friendly, very legible.
- Sizes: S 66, M 150, L 196, XL 236. Slams flash a warm white.
- Feels like: a confident product film; Apple-like restraint, black stage, one warm accent.
- Use for: product and brand lines, launches, claims with one payoff word.

## ink-green (from the owner's kinetic-type reference)

- Near-black background with a soft deep-green glow in the centre. Text white; accent mint green (#2FE6A0).
- Font: Montserrat, ExtraBold for M and L, Black for XL, SemiBold for S. Geometric, punchy.
- Sizes: S 64, M 142, L 186, XL 226. Tighter leading than night-gold.
- Feels like: the classic kinetic-typography tutorial showcase; energetic, dense blocks.
- Use for: hooks, explainers, quotes, fast social edits.

## paper (light editorial)

- Warm off-white background (#F1F0EC). Text near-black (#15161A); accent orange-red (#FF4A1C).
- Font: Geist, Bold for M and L, Black for XL, SemiBold for S. Neutral, modern grotesk.
- Sizes: S 62, M 140, L 184, XL 224.
- Feels like: a design studio's manifesto; calm and sharp.
- Use for: statements, principles, B2B, anything that should feel considered rather than loud.

## Not available in this version

- Right-to-left scripts (Arabic): the engine does not shape them yet. Write Latin-script films only.
- Images, icons and shapes: this engine is type only.
