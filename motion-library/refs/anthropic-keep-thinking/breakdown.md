## 4. Signature techniques
- **Transitions:** mostly hard cuts on the beat grid (41% of cuts on the grid; 21% on a strong onset vs 15% chance, lift ×1.39). One clear motion-blur whip at 00:37.06. One black turn at 00:15.4. Detector flagged 24 "graphic-match" cuts, but at 00:41.66, 00:46.06, 01:04.43, 01:09.32, 01:14.84, 01:21.40 and 01:22.64 the before/after frames are the same image (in-shot changes such as the UI chip appearing): false positives.
- **Typography:** the brand serif, small and centred ("Keep thinking."), and a single serif word ("Keep") paired with a sans UI chip. The only big type is the found word PROBLEMS inside the footage.
- **Camera:** mostly locked-off or inner motion (62.8% combined); real lenses, real people.
- **Colour and background:** film-stock warmth with teal shadows; mixed native aspect ratios (4:3 and scope letterbox inside 16:9, e.g. shots #13, #14, #53) give an archival collage texture.
- **Pacing:** 79 shots, ASL 1.14 s (median 0.84), decelerating curve [0.73 → 1.61]; the only long holds are the breath frame (3.3 s) and the ending (7.4 s).
- **Sound:** music-led, −14.1 LUFS, LRA 4.2 LU (compressed, steady), true peak −1.1 dBFS. Hits on 32/78 cuts. Voice present in 38% of runtime but only 23 words (≈40 wpm): sparse, calm, mid register (median F0 ≈ 182 Hz; gender UNVERIFIED).

## 5. When to use this style
- **Fits:** AI-lab brand films, tools whose value is "you think better" (BeQuite's harness is exactly this: invisible infrastructure behind people's work); 60–90 s hero film; 16:9.
- **Avoid when:** the viewer must learn a workflow; short social cuts under 20 s (the turn needs time).
- **Cost and difficulty:** the craft is curation: 70+ plates of real people and found text (stock, archive, shot footage; AI only for small plates). One UI chip comp in AE. Medium-high effort, low tech risk.

## 6. What to steal (adapt, never copy)
1. **Product as microcopy on real work:** BeQuite's own status words ("Planning", "Verifying", "Gate passed") as a chip on real developers' screens and hands. Serif lead word fixed, chip word swaps per cut.
2. **One-word antithesis:** two VO lines that differ by one word, split by a black frame.
3. **Find the problem word in the world** (on a terminal, a sticky note, a whiteboard, a log line) and cut it on the beat for 10–15 s.
4. **Held breath frame:** 3 s of an empty plate with 0.4 s of silence right after the fastest section.
5. **End card over a human shot**, then 2 s of silence.
