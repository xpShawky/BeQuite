## 4. Signature techniques
- **Transitions:** few hard cuts (11); scene changes ride on the glass rings (a ring expands from the product and becomes the next stage), the iridescent smear of the title, and tone morphs (black silhouette → white product). Whoosh-in at 4 of 11 cuts. One confirmed same-spot figure cut (14.42); the 32.07 tag is a loose graphic match.
- **Typography:** small (≈2-3 % frame height) black grotesk on white, set beside the object or inside a ring, never over the dancers; coloured only once ("Hello" in purple).
- **Camera:** inner-motion 65 %, locked 30 %: the dancers and the rings move, the camera barely does.
- **Colour and background:** pure white stage, black silhouettes, colour only as thin rainbow fringes on glass (dispersion) and one green battery bolt.
- **Pacing:** ASL by quarter 4.0 / 2.0 / 4.0 / 4.0 s (detector). The rhythm is carried by the choreography, not the cutting.
- **Sound:** −15.6 LUFS, **LRA 2.7 LU** (the most compressed track of the eight), true peak −1.7 dBFS; 129.2 BPM with confidence **3.36** (the steadiest beat in the set). Cuts on strong onsets ×0.78 and on low booms ×0.58: the cuts avoid the hits. The only grid signal is weak: detector cuts cluster at one phase of the half-beat (Rayleigh R = 0.53, p = 0.044, n = 11). Picture-change vs sound cross-correlation z = 1.8 (the weakest of the set). No voice.

## 5. When to use this style
- Fits: audio, wellness and lifestyle products; anything whose benefit is invisible (sound, silence, a sensor) and needs a visual metaphor; 30-50 s; works in 9:16 because silhouettes crop well.
- Avoid when: the audience needs to see real UI or workflow; when you cannot afford choreography and a clean silhouette shoot.
- Cost and difficulty: high. A dance shoot on white (or CG figures), glass/dispersion renders (Redshift / Octane / Blender Cycles with dispersion), compositing rings around moving bodies. The title smear is cheap (AE displacement + chromatic split).

## 6. What to steal (adapt, never copy)
1. **Give an invisible feature a shape and reuse it:** one ring language for ANC, heart rate and hearing. For BeQuite: one "gate" ring for every check (tests, evidence, release).
2. **Title liquefies into the film's material** in 0.2-0.3 s as the opening transition.
3. **Silhouette-to-product tone morph:** the product appears first as a black shape like the people, then lightens into itself.
4. **Word swap around the product** ("Hola" | product | "Hello") for any before/after or input/output feature.
