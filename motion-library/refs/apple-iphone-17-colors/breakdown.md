## 5. When to use this style
- **Fits:** feature lists for a friendly product, colourways, social ads on a light background, 30–60 s.
- **Avoid when:** you want drama or a "most powerful" claim (it reads gentle, not mighty).
- **Cost and difficulty:** low–medium. AE text animators (word swap, weight-in, typing), clean product renders on grey, one licensed upbeat track.

## 6. What to steal (adapt, never copy)
1. **Colour the key word in the product's colour** (for BeQuite: the gate colour of the command being named).
2. **In-place word swap** inside a fixed sentence ("Main" → "Ultra Wide").
3. **Inline product inside a sentence:** a UI window sits between two words of the line.
4. **A short hole in the music** (0.5 s) right before a key line.
