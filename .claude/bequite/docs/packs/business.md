# The business pack

Turning skills and finished work into income, honestly. 5 commands and 2 skills, on top of core.

## What it does

- **`/bq-offer`**: an offer pack (target client, pain and outcome, deliverables with exclusions, price tiers, a guarantee the seller controls, outreach, a demo idea); `proposal` for one client or job post; `local-business` for a minimum digital system for an offline business; `pain-radar` for public pain turned into opportunities; `business-system`, `agency`, `service-business` for a whole service business.
- **`/bq-make-money`**: legitimate earning routes for the user's country, checked on each platform's own pages (eligibility, payout method, fees, minimums), turned into a seven-day plan. Skill: make-money.
- **`/bq-job-finder`**: real work opportunities in the user's languages, checked for scams and country fit, with short pitches. Skill: job-finder.
- **`/bq-start`**: how to start (a personal profile or a brand, which platforms, one niche or pillars).
- **`/bq-productize`**: a finished, evidenced piece of work into a product: a service offer, a template or skill pack, a case study built only from the work's evidence, landing copy, a demo script, a price test.

## Rules that do not bend

No invented demand, no income promises, no claimed experience that is not in memory or the user's input, no legal advice, and nothing sent or submitted by the agent. Scams and terms violations are refused.
