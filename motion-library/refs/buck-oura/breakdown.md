## 1. The story in one line
A whole brand campaign (new colours, ceramic material, the "Ring true to you" line, OOH, film) in 40 seconds. It feels soft, premium and personal.

## 2. Story shape
Logo → product macro → product facts in type → manifesto → hero line → concept-to-creation (shoot, grids) → campaign in the world (OOH) → colour names → second line → live-action film → BTS grid → product hero. A case study: what we made, shown as a rhythm.

## 4. Signature techniques
- **Transitions:** motion-carried curves on the ring (2.09 s), graphic-match triptychs, 8 white flashes, hand-to-hand gesture cuts.
- **Typography:** a soft serif for the hero line, a small monospace for facts and manifesto, both quiet; type never shouts.
- **Camera:** macro 3D ring turns, live action with natural light, flat stills for BTS.
- **Colour and background:** pastel gradients matched to the four ring colours; each section borrows one colour.
- **Pacing:** ASL 0.78 s, median 0.58 s; curve [0.73, 0.86, 0.57, 0.86]: fast and even.
- **Sound:** music only, −12.4 LUFS, **LRA 1.2 LU** (a flat wall: a sizzle has no dynamics), 129 BPM; lift ×1.25; ends in 1.4 s of silence.

## 5. When to use this style
- Fits: case-study reels, portfolio pieces, campaign recaps, rebrand reveals; 30–60 s.
- Avoid when: telling one story or explaining a product; it is a montage of a system.
- Cost and difficulty: needs the campaign assets first; the reel itself is a medium edit with type cards. Product macro in 3D (Blender/C4D/Octane), OOH mock-ups in Photoshop/AE.

## 6. What to steal (adapt, never copy)
1. **Name = world:** each BeQuite command shown in the "place" its name suggests (`/bq-research` in a library, `/bq-verify` under a microscope light).
2. **Fixed hero line over changing hands/shots.**
3. **Small mono fact cards** for numbers ("55 commands · 35 skills · 0 dependencies").
4. **OOH/in-the-world montage** to show a brand system is real (README, terminal, docs site, social post).
5. **Hand-to-hand gesture across cuts** for a "together" beat.
