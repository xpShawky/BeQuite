## 1. The story in one line
Whatever you see, feel, crave or are stuck on, circle it and search it. It feels light, human and curious.

## 2. Story shape
Title → a list of human moments, each a question ("Feeling … it?", "Curious about it?", "Stuck on it?", "Craving it?", "Kind of shook by it?") answered by a gesture ("Circle it.", "Squiggle it.", "Highlight", "Search it.") → "And then get back to it." → title with the gesture → logo. A call-and-response list.

## 4. Signature techniques
- **Transitions:** gesture-colour ribbon wipe (11.9 s), finger-to-lens light wipe (27.6–29.4 s), 6 white flashes, 2 motion-carried cuts, cut on action (foot step).
- **Typography:** Google Sans, white over picture or dark on white, a question built around the product ("Feeling [phone] it?"), short imperative answers ("Circle it."). Hold times ≈ 1.2–2.5 s per line.
- **Camera:** handheld-feeling live action, push-ins 19 %, one top-down.
- **Colour and background:** warm natural live action; Google's four colours only in the gesture strokes and glows.
- **Pacing:** ASL 2.28 s (much slower than the other MvsM films), median 2.0 s; curve [3.07, 1.54, 2.20, 1.92].
- **Sound:** music only, −14.4 LUFS, LRA 3.2 LU, 123 BPM; on-onset 31 % vs chance 20 % (lift ×1.54); 42 % on the beat grid. Cuts follow the beat more than in the word-led films.

## 5. When to use this style
- Fits: one-gesture features, consumer apps, Android/iOS features, "use it anywhere" stories; 30–60 s.
- Avoid when: no people/footage budget; developer audiences that want to see depth.
- Cost and difficulty: medium-high: a live-action shoot with several talents and locations, UI composites on phone screens, AE for type and gradient strokes.

## 6. What to steal (adapt, never copy)
1. **Screen as the missing word:** "Planning [terminal] it?" with the BeQuite terminal in the gap.
2. **Question → imperative rhythm:** "Stuck on a bug? /bq-fix it." "Lost the plan? /bq-plan it."
3. **Finger-to-lens wipe:** a hand presses toward camera, the glow becomes the next title.
4. **End card performs the feature:** the BeQuite wordmark checked off like a gate, or typed as a command.
5. **The gesture's colour is the transition.**
