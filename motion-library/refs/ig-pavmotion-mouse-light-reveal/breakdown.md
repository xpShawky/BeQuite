## 4. Signature techniques
- **Transitions:** 6 hard cuts, ASL 2.54 s, curve [2.22, 1.48, 1.48, 2.22]: calm, symmetrical. One motion-carried cut at 5.12 s.
- **Camera:** slow pull-outs (24.5 %) and slow orbits; macro depth of field.
- **Light:** monochrome grey palette; the product is revealed by a moving key/rim light, not by camera moves. Floor reflections.
- **Voice:** one 2-4 word line per 2 s slot (86 wpm while speaking, very slow), low male. 5 of 6 cuts sit mid-line: the voice is laid on a grid, not cut to.
- **Sound:** −14.3 LUFS, 136 BPM (confidence 2.24), cuts on onsets 17 % vs chance 14 % (lift ×1.2, weak).

## 5. When to use this style
- Fits: hardware and physical product reveals, premium "hero object" moments, 15-30 s.
- Avoid when: the product is software only (use a 3D logo or device instead).
- Cost: medium-high. Blender/C4D + Octane/Redshift/Cycles: one model, an HDRI-free studio of area lights animated, volumetrics, a ghost/X-ray material pass. 3-5 days.

## 6. What to steal
1. **Silhouette → rim light → full form**: animate the light, not the camera.
2. **X-ray / ghost-shell pass** to show the inside of a "black box" (BeQuite's hidden gates and skills).
3. **One short claim per 2 s shot**, low calm VO.
4. **HUD callout lines** drawn onto the hero object.
